> **Scan any food. Know exactly what's in it. No guessing.**

Satvora AI is a multimodal food intelligence app for Android that combines AI visual scanning, barcode reading, and nutritional label OCR to give you instant, accurate nutrition facts — built specifically for Indian and global cuisines.

---

## 🔍 What Satvora Does

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 📸 **AI Photo Scanner** | Point your camera at any dish — home-cooked, restaurant, or street food — and get instant calorie + macro estimates |
| 🏷️ **Barcode Scanner** | Scan any UPC/EAN barcode on packaged goods for instant nutrition facts and ingredient lists |
| 📄 **Label OCR Scanner** | Photograph any printed Nutrition Facts panel and Satvora reads it directly — no barcode required |
| 🧪 **Ingredient Checker** | Flag ultra-processed additives, emulsifiers, artificial sweeteners, and allergens from ingredient lists |
| 🍛 **Indian Food Intelligence** | Calibrated against ICMR-NIN Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 for accurate tracking of dal, roti, biryani, and regional cuisines |
| 🤖 **AI Health Coach** | Personalized dietary guidance in 14 languages based on your health goals and metabolic profile |
| 📊 **Nutrition Vault** | On-device calorie diary, macro tracker, and nutrient history — all stored locally on your device |
| 🔐 **Privacy-First** | Your health data never leaves your device. Local Room SQLite storage. Zero mandatory cloud upload. |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Mobile App (Android)**
- Language: Kotlin
- Architecture: MVVM + Clean Architecture
- Local Database: Room SQLite (offline-first)
- AI/ML: Multimodal computer vision (Google AI)
- Health Integration: Android Health Connect

**Website**
- Framework: Next.js 14 (App Router)
- Hosting: GitHub Pages + Firebase App Hosting
- SEO: Full JSON-LD schema (Organization, MobileApplication, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList)
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4

**Data Sources**
- USDA FoodData Central (branded foods database)
- ICMR-NIN Indian Food Composition Tables 2017
- Open Food Facts (barcode product database)
- FSSAI Food Safety Standards (Indian food regulations)

## 🔒 Privacy Philosophy

Satvora is built on a local-first privacy architecture:

- ✅ All meal logs stored in on-device Room SQLite database
- ✅ No behavioral ad profiling
- ✅ No third-party data sales
- ✅ Compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR
- ✅ Data deletion available instantly from Settings → Clear Local Database
